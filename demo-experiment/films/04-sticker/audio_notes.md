# 04 Sticker Explainer: audio notes

**Sound world:** a clean tech-explainer bed (marimba ostinato) with crisp, tactile sticker Foley.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo / key | 120 BPM (one sticker per beat), D major: D, G, Bm, A |
| Instruments | sampled marimba 8th-note ostinato (stops at 3.0 s so the peel and motif have air) · plucked bass on the chord changes · warm pad + sub on the landing |
| Sonic logo | marimba D4 → A4 → E5 at 3.50 / 3.75 / 4.00 s; the ninth is doubled by glockenspiel E6 and a low marimba E4 |
| The accent | **4.00 s**: the peeled label leaves the frame, revealing the "Data UX" sticker, and its gloss sweep starts. Marimba + glock + Dadd9 pad + sub. |
| SFX (only what you see) | a sticker slap on every landing (panned to its side of the board, the centre label loudest) · air whooshes on the three whip-pans, direction-matched · a soft whoosh on the pull-out · the long sticker-peel tear (3.15–3.75 s) · paper flip + swish as the flap is tossed |
| Tail | glock/marimba ring + pad; silent at 6.8 s |

**What I'd change with more time:** give each sticker a slap with a different "material" (vinyl vs paper vs foam); add a tiny UI "tick" as the cell highlight and the SELECT caret appear; and record a real peel.
