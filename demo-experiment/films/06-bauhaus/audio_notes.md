# 06 Bauhaus: audio notes

**Sound world:** minimal techno / click, mechanical and rational. **No kick drum at all**: the pulse comes from the clicks of shapes landing.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo / key | 120 BPM (0.5 s/beat = the grid's beat), D (major pentatonic clicks over D, Bm, G dub stabs) |
| Instruments | sine blips (one per circle landing, pitched by its height in the scatter) · rim clicks cascading across the columns as each row of squares stacks · square-wave blips per row · a ticking 16th hat only while the poster builds (0.5–3.0 s) · three dub stabs + sub on section downbeats |
| Sonic logo | FM glock-bell + sine blip, D4 → A4 → E5 at 3.50 / 3.75 / 4.00 s, with a dub delay (3/16) tail |
| The accent | **4.00 s**: U and X complete the type. Dadd9 dub stab + 808 sub + rim. The delay tail is the only thing left in the hold. |
| SFX (only what you see) | a scan sweep as the grid draws on · a tick when the axes finish · a sci whoosh as the shaft shoots up · a swish per letter group turning, plus toggles on the D, T and U landings |
| Tail | the delay echoes decay; silent from ~6.0 s |

**What I'd change with more time:** derive every click pitch from the shape's grid column (left→right = low→high) so the poster is literally "played"; and try a single detuned bell for the ninth for a slightly warmer landing.
