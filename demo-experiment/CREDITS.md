## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1; licence texts in `fonts/OFL-*.txt`)
Downloaded from github.com/google/fonts, instanced to static weights and subset to basic Latin + kerning with fontTools
(modification is permitted by the OFL). Orbitron declares a Reserved Font Name, so its modified subset is renamed
**"DUX Orbit"** (`fonts/DUXOrbit-Black.ttf`); none of the other fonts declare one.

| Font | Used in |
|---|---|
| Caveat Bold | 01 Cel Boil: the hand-lettered "Data UX" (outlines boiled by code) |
| Outfit Bold | 03 Morph: letter shapes the rows fold into |
| Poppins Black | 04 Sticker: the "Data UX" sticker |
| JetBrains Mono Bold | 04 Sticker: "SELECT *" |
| Playfair Display Black, Anton, Old Standard TT | 05 Collage: ransom-note letters; Old Standard also prints the stock-table digits |
| Orbitron Black (shipped renamed as DUX Orbit) | 07 Synthwave: chrome "DATA UX" |

02 Line Art and 06 Bauhaus use no font: their letters are drawn from hand-authored paths / geometric primitives.
