# 05 Collage: audio notes

**Sound world:** vinyl crackle and a lazy lo-fi jazz bed. The cut-and-paste Foley is the rhythm section.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo / key | 96 BPM with light swing for the bed (cut points sit on the 12 fps stop-motion frames); D major: Dmaj9, Bm9, Gmaj9, A13sus |
| Instruments | FM Rhodes comping · Karplus-Strong upright bass · brush snare only on scrap landings (5 hits, not a groove) · vinyl crackle bed |
| Sonic logo | Rhodes D4 → A4 → E5 at 3.50 / 3.75 / 4.00 s (the second "a", the "U" and the "X" ransom letters); the ninth is doubled by vibraphone |
| The accent | **4.00 s**: the X ransom letter is pasted down. A rubber-stamp thump, Rhodes Dadd9 with an upright D2 under it, and the vibes E5. |
| SFX (only what you see) | cloth swish as the red band slides in · scissors snip before each big scrap, then a paper slide + slap as it lands · tape rip on the neck tape · slap for the ruler and the hand, a swish as the ruler-arm swings up · marker scribbles while the axes and the red line are drawn · a slap per ransom letter |
| Tail | Rhodes ring + crackle; mastered fade to silence at 6.8 s |

**What I'd change with more time:** a short "breath" mouth sound when the eye is pasted on (the person comes alive); real recorded paper slaps (the synthesized ones are a bit uniform); and a muted trumpet answer to the motif.
