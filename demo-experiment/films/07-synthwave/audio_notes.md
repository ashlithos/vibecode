# 07 Synthwave: audio notes

**Sound world:** a 1986 synthwave title sequence: tape hiss, saw bass, lush pads, gated-reverb snare.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo / key | 120 BPM, D minor: i–VI–III–VII (Dm, Bb, F, C) |
| Instruments | 16th-note saw-bass arpeggio (root/octave, filter opening as the camera flies) · 80s poly pad · saw pluck arpeggio · gated-reverb snare **only on three beats** (1, 2, 3 s; 2 s is when the pie slices part). No kick, no four-on-the-floor. |
| Sonic logo | 80s lead D4 → A4 → E5 at 3.50 / 3.75 / 4.00 s |
| The accent | **4.00 s**: chrome DATA UX lands and the VHS tracking glitch hits. A big Dm(add9) chord (pad + supersaw D-A-E), saw sub D1, gated snare and crash, plus a 0.27 s tape stutter. |
| SFX (only what you see) | VHS noise + CRT on under the tracking-noise open · head-switch click when tracking locks (0.45 s) · a low whoosh under the title rise · the tape glitch on the hit · a sparkle with the star glint |
| Tail | pad and crash decay; silent at 6.8 s |

**What I'd change with more time:** a proper Juno-style chorus on the pad and a DX-style bass for authenticity; sync the bass arp's filter to the terrain height under the camera.
