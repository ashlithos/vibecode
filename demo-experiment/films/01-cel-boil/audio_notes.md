# 01 Cel Boil: audio notes

**Sound world:** jazzy pizzicato + xylophone, cartoon Foley. Everything is acoustic-sounding and a little bouncy, like the boiling ink.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo / key | 120 BPM (all actions on the 8th-note grid), D major |
| Instruments | Karplus-Strong pizzicato bass (enters with the first bar), pizz chord "chucks", FM xylophone plinks (one per bar landing, rising F#4→D5), marimba-sample doubling on the motif, glockenspiel + vibraphone on the landing |
| Sonic logo | D4 (3.50 s, letter **t** lands) → A4 (3.75 s, **U** lands) → E5 (4.00 s, **X** lands): xylophone, with a marimba doubling on the ninth |
| The accent | **4.00 s / frame 120**: the X lands and the underline snaps in. A Dadd9 pizz chord strums (D2-D3-A3-E4-F#4), with glockenspiel E6, a vibraphone D5 and a sparkle that matches the star burst. It is the loudest 50 ms of the mix (~9 dB above the bed). |
| SFX (only what you see) | swish as the cursor flies in · marker scribble while each axis is drawn · stretchy rise + mouth-pop per bar · mouse click · cork-pop + soft whoosh on the "poof" · soft pops for the D and first a landing · swish as the cursor exits |
| Tail | the landing chord and vibraphone ring out; mastered fade to digital silence at 6.8 s |

**What I'd change with more time:** record a real upright pizz and a real xylophone (the synth/KS versions are slightly toy-like); give each smoke puff its own tiny "poof" instead of one shared whoosh; and try brushed-snare ghost notes under the bar growth for more swing, as long as it never becomes a beat.
